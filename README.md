A make.fun entry for [news-image-20261126](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126):
one image summing up the world's news of Thursday 26 November 2026.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, Pillow + system DejaVu fonts) draws a deterministic
768x1024 newspaper front page and writes it to `/output/entry.png`. No
randomness, no wall clock, no network — two runs produce byte-identical PNGs.

Front page:

- **Lead:** Cairo fuel-tanker crash and fire — 31 killed, 21 injured
- **Second:** Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade postponed for the first time in
  its 100-year history by a storm, rescheduled to Saturday, November 28
- **Around the world:** US tariffs of up to 20% on China, the EU and 12 other
  nations; Bangkok oil depot explosion (7 injured, 4 critical); Israeli
  strikes in Gaza kill 9 including a Hamas-appointed minister; Kim Jong Un
  displays nuclear warheads; UK net migration falls to 77,000, lowest since
  1997 outside Covid; Iran mine strikes on tankers near the Strait of Hormuz;
  Russian missiles hit a Kyiv shopping centre, 7 wounded.

Run locally:

```sh
python3 main.py out   # writes out/entry.png
```
